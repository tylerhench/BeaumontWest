How a project runs

Four steps, two to four weeks

Open any step to see what happens and what we need from you.

01Complete the intake form

Share your business details, services and photos so the site is tailored to you. On Standard and Premium we write your page copy from it, so the more you share, the more it sounds like you.

02Review a sample page

We design one page first, so you can confirm the direction before the rest is built. On Starter, that page is your whole site.

03Refine and build out

We incorporate your feedback, then carry it through the remaining pages. Most projects are completed in two to four weeks, depending on the package and how quickly we receive your intake form, photos and final approval.

04Launch

We point your domain, test on real phones and go live. Then it runs on your own hosting account, or we look after it on a care plan.

Common questions

Before you call

How much does a site cost?

Each package has a fixed price, so you'll know the full cost before we start. Ask for the price sheet on the contact page, or we'll go through it on a quick call.

How does payment work?

Half to start and half at launch. Premium can also be split into smaller payments.

Do I need a care plan?

No. Without one, we set you up with your own hosting account and hand over your site's files at launch. From then on, upkeep and edits are yours. Either way, your domain is registered in your name.

I don't have any good photos.

Common, and fixable. We'll work with what's on your phone, or build the design around type and colour so it doesn't lean on photography at all. What we won't do is fill your site with stock images of someone else's business.

What if I already have a site?

Then the first question is whether it needs rebuilding or just fixing. Sometimes a day of cleanup beats a new site, and we'll tell you that even though it's the smaller invoice. If we do rebuild, your existing pages get redirected so you don't lose the search rankings you've built up.

Not sure which package fits?

That's what the call is for. Tell us about the business and we'll recommend one, including the smaller one if it'll do.
